Why CampusSetu?

Built to Solve College Infrastructure Friction

Most college complaint forms end up in a black hole with duplicate tickets, leaked contact numbers, and zero transparency. CampusSetu changes that.

🤖

Smart Duplicate Detection

Real-time heuristic detects if another student already reported the same Wi-Fi glitch, broken water cooler, or lab issue. Students can simply upvote rather than cluttering administrative queues.

Automated Privacy Guard

How It Works

A 3-Step Collaborative Resolution Cycle

Streamlined for students and transparent for campus representatives.

1

Report & Auto-Sanitize

Choose your campus location and describe the problem. Our real-time engine flags duplicates and redacts private contact details instantly.

2

Peer Verification & Upvoting

Students facing the exact same outage or damage tap "Facing this" to build community weight and weed out non-issues.

3

Audit Log & Resolution

Track transparent progress updates. Verified demo entries show realistic milestones without false administrative claims.
